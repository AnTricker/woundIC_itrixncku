[CATEGORY]
Ingrown Nail / Nail-Fold Lesion

[POSITIVE CUES]
- Nail plate edge interacting with lateral or proximal nail fold.
- Periungual erythema, edema, swelling, crust, discharge, pus, or granulation tissue.
- Fold hypertrophy or skin overlapping the nail edge.
- Nail discoloration or deformity only if visible.

[NEGATIVE REPORTING]
- If nail-fold swelling is absent, report it as "not observed".
- If pus/discharge is absent, report it as "not observed".
- If the nail edge cannot be seen, put nail-edge relationship under cannot_determine.

[CONFUSABLE CATEGORIES]
- Cut/laceration: open traumatic wound away from nail fold.
- Bruise: intact subcutaneous discoloration without nail-fold inflammation.
- General paronychia: nail-fold inflammation may be present without clear ingrown nail edge.

[FORBIDDEN INFERENCE]
- Do not diagnose infection; describe visible purulence, erythema, or edema only.
- Do not infer diabetes, fungal disease, or chronicity unless directly visible.

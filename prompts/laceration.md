[CATEGORY]
Laceration

[POSITIVE CUES]
- Jagged, irregular, torn skin margins.
- Tissue flap, avulsion-like opening, or uneven wound bed.
- Tissue bridging may be visible across the wound.
- Surrounding contusion, edema, or abrasion may support blunt tearing.

[NEGATIVE REPORTING]
- If margins are clean and sharply linear, report conflict with laceration and possible cut/incision appearance.
- If tissue bridging is not visible, list it under expected_but_not_observed.
- If surrounding contusion is absent, report it as "not observed".

[CONFUSABLE CATEGORIES]
- Cut/incision: clean linear edge and sharp alignment.
- Stab/puncture: small entry hole, depth cue greater than width.
- Abrasion: superficial friction injury without torn margins.

[FORBIDDEN INFERENCE]
- Do not infer mechanism, object, contamination, or need for closure.
- Do not infer depth without visible tissue cues.

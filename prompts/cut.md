[CATEGORY]
Cut / Incision

[POSITIVE CUES]
- Linear or sharply defined skin break.
- Clean, aligned, or smooth wound edges.
- Narrow gap or gaping line with visible blood/clot only if present.
- Shape may be straight, curved, V-shaped, or slit-like.

[NEGATIVE REPORTING]
- If edges are jagged/torn rather than clean, report conflict with cut/incision.
- If there is no active bleeding or exudate, report it as "not observed".
- If depth cannot be determined from the image, put it under cannot_determine.

[CONFUSABLE CATEGORIES]
- Laceration: jagged torn margins, tissue bridging, tissue flap, associated contusion.
- Stab/puncture: small entry point with depth cue greater than width.
- Abrasion: superficial scraped surface rather than a clean edge split.

[FORBIDDEN INFERENCE]
- Do not infer the object that caused the cut unless visible.
- Do not recommend sutures or treatment.

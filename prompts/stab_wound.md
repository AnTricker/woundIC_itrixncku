[CATEGORY]
Stab Wound / Puncture Wound

[POSITIVE CUES]
- Small entry point, slit-like opening, round puncture, or narrow wound aperture.
- Depth cue may appear greater than surface width, but only report if visually supported.
- Local erythema, edema, bruising, or bleeding may be present.
- Multiple puncta or paired puncture marks only if visible.

[NEGATIVE REPORTING]
- If the wound is broad and torn, report conflict with stab/puncture and possible laceration.
- If no depth cue is visible, put depth under cannot_determine.
- If no surrounding bruising/swelling is visible, report it as "not observed".

[CONFUSABLE CATEGORIES]
- Cut/incision: longer clean linear split.
- Laceration: jagged torn opening with tissue flap or bridging.
- Abrasion: superficial scraped area without a puncture entry point.

[FORBIDDEN INFERENCE]
- Do not infer weapon/object, internal damage, or emergency severity.
- Do not infer depth or trajectory beyond visible surface cues.

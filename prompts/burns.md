[CATEGORY]
Burns

[POSITIVE CUES]
- Erythematous burned skin, blistering, bullae, peeled epidermis, moist wound bed, waxy surface, leathery eschar, or charred tissue.
- Thermal pattern may be diffuse, sharply demarcated, splash-like, contact-shaped, or irregular.
- Periwound erythema and edema may be present.

[NEGATIVE REPORTING]
- If blisters/bullae are not visible, explicitly list "blistering/bullae" under expected_but_not_observed.
- If eschar or necrosis is not visible, report necrosis_eschar as "not observed".
- If burn depth cannot be visually supported, put depth under cannot_determine.

[CONFUSABLE CATEGORIES]
- Abrasion: friction scrape without thermal blistering/waxy texture.
- Bruise: intact discoloration without blistering or wound bed.
- Cut/laceration: linear or torn tissue separation rather than thermal surface injury.

[FORBIDDEN INFERENCE]
- Do not assign burn degree unless visible evidence is strong; prefer visual descriptors over degree labels.
- Do not infer cause, temperature, or timing.

[CATEGORY]
Bruises / Contusions

[POSITIVE CUES]
- Intact skin with subcutaneous discoloration.
- Purple, blue, red, yellow-green, brown, or mottled chromatic pattern.
- Diffuse or patchy margins rather than open wound edges.
- Swelling or hematoma-like elevation only if directly visible.

[NEGATIVE REPORTING]
- If the epidermis is broken, report that this conflicts with a pure bruise.
- If no open wound, bleeding, exudate, or crust is visible, mark them "not observed".
- If color staging is unclear, say "cannot determine bruise stage from image".

[CONFUSABLE CATEGORIES]
- Abrasion: scraped epidermis with raw surface.
- Laceration/cut: open tissue separation.
- Burn: blistering, moist wound bed, waxy/leathery texture, or thermal pattern.

[FORBIDDEN INFERENCE]
- Do not date the bruise unless the image clearly supports a cautious color-based estimate.
- Do not diagnose abuse, coagulopathy, or systemic disease.
